Why It Works

Built on behavioral
economics.

Two principles from behavioral economics that make GymROI more motivating than any streak app.

Loss Aversion

People are 2× more motivated to avoid losing money than to gain equivalent value. Seeing "$25/session" framed as money already wasted is more powerful than any streak counter.

Sunk Cost Reframing

Instead of letting your monthly fee fade into the background noise, GymROI keeps it front and center. The money is already spent. The only variable is how many sessions you split it across.
